# Roadmap & Priorities (updated Nov 15 2026, after the rolling-game rework, the plaza rebuild and the UI pass)

## Live in the "Golden Hills" build (one app: lobby + golf, published artifact)
- **Lobby:** plaza (tee board, pro shop wardrobe, Birdie Ball machine, fountain, **blackjack table with a dealer**, **Street Dealer**), 8 RV lots (claimed per player), each with a **mailbox**, GTA-style movement, chat, presence, F to flop (ragdoll), sprint-bonks, fountain trips. **T** opens the trophy case, **B** (on your own lot) opens Build Mode, **J** opens your stash. The lobby camera pulls in rather than looking through RVs, the pro shop, the tee board or garages. A **wallet pill** (top left) shows your cash, Birdie Dollars and unread mail.
- **Controls panel, options and welcome (Nov 15):** see `ui-and-controls.md`.
  - **Controls panel:** a card down the right-hand side always shows what the keys and mouse do right now (on foot, driving, Build Mode, blackjack, each step of rolling, lining up a shot, swinging, putting…). It replaces the old one-line hints.
  - **Options menu:** **Esc** or the gear in the corner. Volumes for everything, ambience and game sounds; shadows on or off; picture sharpness; mouse look speed; the controls panel on or off. On the course it also has **Leave the round**.
  - **Welcome card:** shown once on a first visit, saying where things are.
- **Plaza rebuilt (Nov 15):** the pro shop is a clapboard hut with a tin roof, a serving hatch with a striped awning, a lit sign and a mirror; the tee board stands under its own shingled roof with a planter and a golf bag; the benches are slatted, on iron ends. Wood is from two Poly Haven photos.
- **World feel (two passes, Nov 15):** see `world-feel.md`.
  - **Ambience everywhere:** a low city hum, a far highway with the odd car passing, and now and then a siren, a car horn, a bus's air brakes or a dog across town; under that, light wind, evening crickets, small birds and a dove, a distant train horn. All synthesized; nothing is downloaded. It was thinned out after the first mix was "a bit busy".
  - **Footsteps change with the ground** (concrete, asphalt, gravel, grass, green, sand), in town and on the course. **Other players' footsteps and carts** are heard from where they are.
  - **Your cart** has an electric whine and tyre noise. The **plaza fountain** splashes as you get close.
  - **Wind you can see:** palms lean, oak crowns shiver, grass bends, the flag on the green ripples and string lights swing, all with the gusts you hear.
  - **Sky life:** flocks of birds cross in the distance and call as they pass; a jet goes over now and then with blinking lights, a contrail and a soft roar; a pale moon is up in the east.
  - **Ground life:** small birds peck on the ground and take off when you run or drive at them (town and course); a stray cat in town bolts when you get close; two strollers walk the sidewalk round the plaza.
  - A **speaker button** (bottom right) turns the ambience off and on.
- **World assets (Nov 15):** see `asset-audit.md`.
  - **Photo ground:** real sand in the bunkers, real dirt on the cart paths, and in town real asphalt, concrete pavers, gravel lots and meadow. Tree trunks have photo bark.
  - **The RV is rebuilt:** a rounded travel trailer with curtained windows, a door with a step and porch light, an owner-colour stripe and striped awning, tandem wheels, a hitch with propane bottles, and roof gear.
  - **Golf clubs are rebuilt:** real head shapes for woods, irons, wedges and the putter.
- **Golf betting (Nov 15):** see `golf-betting.md`. On in every group round except Casual.
  - **B** on the course opens the bet menu: pick a stake, then offer Hole winner, Closest to the pin (par 3s), Skins or Round winner (tournaments). Others answer with **Y / N**.
  - An offer is open for up to 20 s or until everyone has answered; nobody tees off while one is open. "Same bets next hole" re-offers and rejoins automatically.
  - **Live shot bets** on the player about to hit: holes it / misses, hits the green / misses. First taker gets the other side.
  - Pots are equal stakes and zero-sum. **Leaving forfeits** your open bets to the players who stay.
  - No server: the proposer alone publishes each roster, and one member publishes each result for everyone to copy (`shared/src/bets.ts`, unit-tested).
- **The city (Nov 14):** a classic pre-war city on the horizon: brick and limestone towers all round and an art-deco downtown toward the sunset, in golden-hour haze. Facades are Poly Haven brick and stone photos; see `asset-audit.md`.
  - **Lobby:** the city sits far out, where the old hills were, so the town keeps its space. Between the town and the city are dry meadows with **oak and palm groves**, and a clear lane out of the gate.
  - **Courses:** the same skyline, taller, behind every hole.
- **Sky and shadows:** a real sunset sky photo, a drifting cloud layer, and cloud shadows sliding over the ground in both the lobby and the course. The sun sits at 12°, and **real cast shadows are back on (Nov 15)**: they had been switched off by a code slip, so trees, palms, buildings and golfers now throw long golden-hour shadows. See `asset-audit.md`.
- **Cash wallet (Nov 14):**
  - **Cash** is the second currency (`street-dealer-economy.md`). It's saved with your progress (this browser + your account record; since Nov 15 the most recently saved copy wins between devices). Every player starts with **$5,000**. Birdie Dollars stay separate.
  - **Ways to earn it:** selling product to the Street Dealer, blackjack, and golf bets.
- **Mailbox (Nov 14):** every lot's mailbox raises its flag when there's unread mail; E opens it. Letters can carry cash to claim. The first letters are a welcome note and a **dev-testing letter from Mom** (she sold your childhood Pokémon cards: **$5,000**). Gifts between players are designed but switched off (`GIFTS_ON`).
- **Blackjack (Nov 14), multiplayer with a dealer:** see `blackjack-multiplayer.md`.
  - **The table:** a hand-built half-moon table under the plaza umbrella: thick wooden apron, printed felt, padded rail, connected legs with brass feet, chip tray, shoe, discard holder, **five stools**. The card shoe sits in the dealer's corner so it doesn't hide the first seat's chips (Nov 15). No free CC0 table model exists.
  - **Five seats, shared rounds:** everyone at the table plays the same shoe against the same dealer and sees everyone's cards and bets. A host (the lowest seated player) runs the clock; every client replays the round from a shared seed, so nobody needs a server.
    - **Timers:** 10 s to bet, **20 s per decision** (was 30 until Nov 15) (auto-stand when it runs out), a short pause after settling.
    - Players sit on the stools in a seated pose, hands on the rail, and others see them there.
  - **Dealer NPC:** a standing dealer behind the table whose hands follow the cards (deal, flip, collect) and who shuffles when the shoe is reshuffled. Between cards he **turns to face whoever's turn it is**.
  - **First-person seat view:** you see the table from your own head, **looking down at your cards**; drag to look around, Shift to lean in, **V** for the over-the-shoulder view.
  - **Controls:** chips $10–$1K (keys 1–5), Space deals or re-bets, Hit (H), Stand (S), Double (D), Split (P). Esc leaves between hands. Table limits $10 to $5,000.
  - **House rules** (`shared/src/blackjack.ts`, `bjtable.ts`, unit-tested): 6-deck shoe reshuffled at 75%, dealer stands on all 17s, blackjack pays 3:2, double on any two cards (also after a split), one split, split aces get one card, no insurance.
  - **Assets:** Kenney's Boardgame Pack cards and chips, Kenney's Casino Audio (both CC0), packed as `public/casino/{cards.png, chips.png, sfx.json}`.
- **Growing + Street Dealer (Nov 14):** see `grow-mechanics.md` and `street-dealer-economy.md`.
  - **Gear in Build Mode:** a grow pot (1 plant) and a grow bed (3 plants, a little faster). Plant one of three strains from the panel; each strain has its own look, time and value.
  - **Plants grow only while you're in the game** and never die. Dry soil pauses growth and loses quality; overwatering risks root rot.
  - **Care v2 (hands-on):** fill a held **watering can** at the spigot (3 pours) and pour it over the plants; buy **fertilizer** at the bin ($25 for 3 doses) and spread it. The bin sits well clear of the spigot so the two prompts don't fight. A coverage ring shows what you'll hit. Plants droop when thirsty, soil darkens when wet, icons show what's needed. **Sprinklers** water everything nearby. **Visitors can water your plants**, and everyone sees what others are holding.
  - **Harvest** into product, then sell it to the **Street Dealer** NPC on the plaza with hold-to-sell. Prices use the agreed clamp (Option B) and the dealer has a strain he wants today, which pays more. He also sells **rolling papers** and a **grinder**. A full grow-to-sale run was tested at $4,424.
- **Grind, Roll & Smoke (Nov 14):** see `grind-roll-smoke.md`.
  - **J** opens your stash. Roll a Skinny, Regular or Fatty through a six-step minigame, **reworked Nov 15** (`rolling-minigame.md`): pinch big buds apart, drag and crease the paper, sprinkle with pinched fingers (what you drop stays), rub to roll, lick in one steady stroke, twist and tap. Each step is timed and earns a medal; the joint is graded **Clean / Decent / Messy**.
  - Light it, then **hold R** to puff: the hand comes up to the lips, the ember glows, and you exhale a cloud. Everyone sees it.
  - The buzz builds with each puff and fades over a couple of minutes: a tinted vignette, colour drift, floating motes, a gentle sway.
  - **H** passes the joint to the nearest player… or, if a garden gnome is closest, something happens (see the doc).
- **Driving in town (Nov 14):** if your lot has a cart garage, press **E** at the garage to take your cart out.
  - **Controls:** W/S drive, A/D steer, Space brake, Shift floor it, E hop out.
  - **Drifting (Nov 15), in town and on the course:** at speed, hold Space while steering. The tail steps out and the cart holds the slide; smoke goes white → blue → orange as a charge builds; letting go gives a short boost (longer when orange). Steering in tightens the slide, steering out widens it.
  - **Kerbs and lot pads are solid** for the cart, so wheels ride up them.
  - **Getting in and out:** E next to the cart gets back in. Hopping out inside your garage parks it. E at the garage while the cart is elsewhere sends it home. E flips it back over if it's on its side.
  - **Collisions:** carts use the same Rapier vehicle as the course. They collide with the plaza props, RVs, fences, palms, every placed lot item (synced as physics bodies) and invisible walls at the town edge.
  - **What others see:** your cart moving, with you seated and hands on the wheel. The garage's parked cart hides while its owner is out. Carts block walkers, and running someone over ragdolls them (both ways).
  - **Leaving for golf** sends the cart home.
- **Build Mode v1 (Nov 14):** see `build-mode.md`.
  - **Entering:** press B on your own lot. You get an orbit camera over the lot (right-drag to turn, scroll to zoom, WASD to pan); nearby street palms hide while you build.
  - **Layout:** a catalog on the left (Furniture, Decoration, Trophies, Garage, Grow; search; thumbnails; prices) and an action bar at the bottom (Rotate Q/E, Grid G, Remove X, Undo Ctrl+Z).
  - **Placing:** click an item, then click the lot. A ghost snaps to a 0.5 m grid (grid off = free placement, 15° rotation). Its footprint turns green or red, with a red tip saying why it can't go there. Click a placed item to pick it up and move it.
  - **On table tops (Nov 15):** small things (trophies, gnomes, pots, lanterns, a cooler, the cat statue) can stand on the picnic table, the café table, the crate, the cooler and the folding stool, and on **the RV's roof and the cart garage's roof**. Point at the top and click; no grid up there. Pick the table up and what was on it drops to the ground.
  - **Post-only items:** **string lights** only block at their two posts, so you can walk under the span and place other items under it.
  - **Catalog:** 19 detailed Poly Haven (CC0) models plus hand-built low-poly pieces, trophies (each once), one cart garage per lot, and grow gear.
  - **Saving and sharing:** layouts save to the shared `plots/{owner}` record (and this browser), so everyone sees each lot as its owner left it.
  - **Cash:** placing costs the item's price; removing refunds it in full; Undo balances out. Anything already on the lot when you open Build Mode counts as paid for.
- **Tournaments (Nov 14):** chosen on the tee board's **Format** row: Single hole, **Mini cup (3 holes)** or **Championship (9 holes)**, solo or as a group.
  - Every tournament comes from one seed carried in the match id, so the whole group gets the same holes, order, cup name and trophy. 🎲 "Another cup" rerolls.
  - **Hole order, "teach then test"** (`shared/src/tournament.ts`): a par template, a different archetype on every hole, an easy opener and a hard finisher.
  - **Cup names** are random and golfy ("The 7th Annual Rusty Cart Cup", "The El Pollo Dorado Cup"…). The event word picks the trophy's topper.
  - **Play:** the HUD shows the cup, "Hole n of N" and a to-par leaderboard; a scorecard after each hole; Birdie Dollars still paid per hole.
  - **Winner only** takes the trophy (ties share; solo needs par or better). Trophies are procedural (`client/src/world/trophy.ts`), live in the trophy case and can be placed on your lot.
- **Avatar (Nov 14): MakeHuman golfer** (CC0 body, clothes, hair; MakeHuman's own 52-bone skeleton with finger rig; `tools/mh/build.py`). Geometry base64 inside golfer.json because the artifact host won't serve .bin. The wardrobe tints everything at runtime.
- **Motion capture (Nov 14):** `tools/mh/mocap.py` retargets CMU subject 64 (golf swing, putt, tee-up, ball pick-up) and the Bandai Namco Research Motion Dataset (walk/run loops, cheer; CC BY-NC 4.0) onto our rig. Idle is still procedural.
- **Sloping lies (Nov 13):** uphill adds loft and costs distance, downhill de-lofts and runs; ball above the feet draws left, below fades right; any slope shrinks the timing window. Ignored under 2%.
- **Golf:** Hole 1 "The Dogleg" + random holes (archetype generator, see below; 5-char hole codes), putting line + green slope arrows, minimap, group rounds (up to 4, **turn-based**; "Everyone at once (casual)" skips turns and betting), ball bonks. The swing is written up in full in `golf-swing-spec.md` ("The swing today").
  - **The swing:** **W/S** set the power (5–100%), then two presses: one to start, one to hit as the marker crosses the zone. No second press calls the swing off.
  - **One-way meter (Nov 15):** the marker runs up the arc once. The hit zone near the top is green (perfect) with orange either side (good). The arc is longer for a bigger shot. It was sped up a little and the green widened a little the same day.
  - **Meter look (Nov 15):** a hand-sprayed sticker: cream border, wobbly marker outlines, pink → orange → yellow paint filling behind a spinning starburst marker, a dashed line down the middle. "HIT!" pops up only on the strike.
  - **Club choice:** the club picked at address is only a suggestion. **Q/E move through every club** from anywhere: driver (off the tee) through the wedge, then the putter's four ranges.
  - **Max power** is 115% for a perfect strike at full power.
  - **Shift overhead:** hold Shift while addressing for a bird's-eye view of the landing ring.
  - **Real cup (Nov 15):** the hole is cut into the green at true size with a 3D cup below. A holed ball drops over the lip, knocks off the liner and the pin, and rattles to rest. Too fast across the hole and it lips out.
  - **Flag (Nov 15):** the flagstick and flag fade out when you're within about 7 m of the hole.
- **Turn-based group rounds (Nov 14):** see `multiplayer-turns.md`. Hole 1 by list order, later holes last place first, then farthest from the pin. A 50 s timer (it waits while a bet offer is open); running out adds a penalty stroke; the third time on a hole picks the ball up. Each player's own cart waits in one of 6 reserved slots behind the tee.
- **Getting around (course):** walk/run to your ball (E to address, R to skip the walk) or drive your own cart (Rapier physics, forgiving crashes, E flips it or hauls it out of water). Synced to everyone.
- **Vehicles framework:** `vehicles.ts` — model + owner-side physics + compact network state that others interpolate.
- **Birdie Dollars + Birdie Balls:** birdie = 1, eagle = 2, ace/albatross = 3. Lobby gacha machine: 1 dollar per spin, never a duplicate. **102 skins in 8 sets** (Nov 15): the five drawn sets, plus **Animals** (30), **Masterpieces** (14 famous paintings) and **Landmarks** (8), which use real CC0 and public-domain art. See `ball-skins.md`.
- **Look:** golden hour; textured turf, per-pixel greens/fringes/bunkers, California live oaks and fan palms, billboard-sprite grass that gets trodden down; balls sit down in long grass.

## Random hole generator (Nov 13, follows the course design philosophy doc)
Each hole starts from a strategic idea; hazards, slopes and widths are placed to serve it. The tee shows the hole's question as a tip.
- **Par 3:** carry, valley, stamp, redan. **Par 4:** cape, corner, split, driveable, ridge. **Par 5:** reachable, double dogleg.
- **Fairness checks:** dry, not-too-steep landing zones; flat pin spots; no cliffs on greens; clear approach; one water carry max.
- **Strategy check:** ~150 simulated aim lines; a hole passes with a dry SAFE line and a riskier, rewarding BOLD line. ~76% pass.
- **Minimap at the tee** marks the SAFE (white) and BOLD (orange) landing spots.
- **Not yet:** wind as part of a hole's design; better split/redan pass rates.
