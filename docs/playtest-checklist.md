# Playtest checklist (Nov 16 2026, build m2.02)

Things built on Nov 16 that nobody has played yet. Tick them off, or note what went wrong. About 30 minutes alone, plus 20 with a second player.

## Alone, in town
- [ ] Walk from the plaza to the West Side. The street climbs a rise. Do you stay on the ground all the way up and down?
- [ ] **Drive a cart up and down the rise.** Does it climb, and does anything catch at the top or bottom?
- [ ] Press F to flop on top of the rise. Do you land on the ground, not under it?
- [ ] The south edge of town: the hills are gone. Can you walk out of the world anywhere?
- [ ] Look round the horizon: mountains behind the skyline on every side.
- [ ] Tab menu, M map (is the West Side in the right place?), Q emotes, I inventory.
- [ ] Esc, then Credits.

## West Side
- [ ] Lounge: sit at a slot machine, pull the lever (Space) until you win something. Does the sign flash and the cash go up by the right amount?
- [ ] Sit at video poker: deal, keep cards with 1 to 5, draw. Check one payout against the table on the screen.
- [ ] V at a machine pulls the camera back; E stands up where you sat down.
- [ ] Blackjack: sit, play three hands, leave.
- [ ] Birdie Ball machine in the lounge: does the close-up camera look right?
- [ ] Dealer in the alley: sell something.
- [ ] Grow Supply: buy a pot and fertilizer. On your lot, open Build Mode and place the pot from the Grow tab. Plant, then take fertilizer out at the bin and use it.
- [ ] Threads (clothing): try a piece you don't own, press Buy and wear. Is the price taken once, and is the piece free next time?
- [ ] Barber and Ink Spot: change hair, add a tattoo.
- [ ] On your own lot: Tab, Mirror. Change build and skin.
- [ ] Pro Shop: buy a ball trail, then play a hole. Is the ball's line that colour?

## On the course
- [ ] A hole starts with you standing beside the ball. E addresses it; T addresses it from anywhere.
- [ ] The swing: faster meter, wider green, and the call-out (PERFECT!, A HAIR EARLY, LATE!). Does the speed feel right?
- [ ] In the cart: R turns the radio on, 1 to 0 change station.
- [ ] Hold a lit joint: arm at your side, joint in the fingers, chest moves when you puff.

## Fun Land
- [ ] Walk round the wheel, carousel, big top and stalls. Anything you can walk through that you shouldn't, or can't walk past?
- [ ] Does the game feel slower there?

## With a second player
- [ ] Do you see each other on the rise at the right height?
- [ ] One sits at a slot machine: does the other see them seated, and is the machine marked IN USE?
- [ ] Blackjack together at the lounge table.
- [ ] One drives with the radio on: does the other hear it nearby?
- [ ] Mini putt at the same time: do you see each other's balls?
- [ ] A group round with a bet.
