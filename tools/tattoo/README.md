# Tattoo sheet
`public/golfer/tattoos.png` is 18 designs from Openclipart (CC0), 256 px cells, 6 across. Black designs are stored as
alpha only (the wardrobe's ink colour tints them); `rose`, `skull` and `sugar` keep their colours.
`src/` holds the screenshots they were cut from. Openclipart ids, in sheet order:
dragon 220726, lion 220731, bull 220727, wings 220728, heart 319654, star 64927, wolf 171726, butterfly 250696,
serpent 168398, lionface 255169, dolphin 217237, rose 4586, sun 284716, skull 352587, skullrose 341837, sugar 191019,
anchor 320131, swallow 329393. Fetch: open `openclipart.org/image/800px/<id>` in the browser and save the screenshot.
